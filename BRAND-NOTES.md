## Header lockup
The approved public-site lockup pairs the Convera Strategies logo directly with **People are what we do.** beneath it. The tagline should not be repeated as ordinary page-hero or body copy. Future stationery and business-card work should preserve this relationship unless the brand system is intentionally revised.

## Slogan treatment
On the public site, **People are what we do.** should read as a slogan rather than a loose line of copy. In the header lockup it is set in a restrained italic serif treatment aligned beneath the wordmark portion of the logo without decorative rules or surrounding lines. The same relationship should guide future business cards and stationery: logo first, slogan visibly attached as the brand promise, never floating elsewhere as decorative copy.

## Name usage
Use **Convera Strategies** for formal identification, SEO, structured data, page titles, first substantive references on company/service pages, contracts, proposals, and stationery. Use **Convera** as the conversational short form within body copy after the full name is established. Avoid mechanically repeating either form in every heading or sentence.
