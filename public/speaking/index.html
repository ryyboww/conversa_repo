Topics

Areas for conversation

These are starting points rather than fixed speeches. Programs can be shaped for professional organizations, universities, public institutions, community audiences, podcasts, panels, classrooms, and moderated discussions.

Speaking inquiries begin through Contact. An invitation is reviewed before scheduling or project Intake is offered.

Institutions, legitimacy & public authority

How organizations explain decisions, exercise authority, and communicate legitimacy when conduct or outcomes are contested.

Law, justice & legal meaning

How legal rules operate through institutional interpretation, procedural choices, records, and the language of courts and justice institutions.

Workplace culture & institutional narratives

How organizations describe conduct, responsibility, reliability, and expectations—and what those descriptions reveal about institutional priorities.

Research through records

Using archival, administrative, legal, and narrative data to examine institutions without reducing complex cases to counts alone.

Public life, trust & meaning

How public confidence, language, social expectations, and shared interpretations shape institutional relationships.

Research-to-practice conversations

Translating careful inquiry into questions organizations can use to examine systems, communication, process, and decision-making.
