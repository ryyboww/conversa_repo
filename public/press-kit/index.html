Extended biography

Approved extended bio

Ryan Brown is founder of Convera Strategies and a doctoral researcher in Criminal Justice and Socio-Legal Studies at Rutgers University–Newark. His scholarly and professional interests center on law, criminal justice, socio-legal studies, institutional narratives, public authority, legitimacy, workplace culture, and organizational meaning.

His work examines how legal and public institutions construct meaning through language, records, decisions, and institutional practices. He is particularly interested in how organizations interpret contested events, explain the exercise of authority, and transform complex social and legal circumstances into institutional narratives. His research approaches records not simply as repositories of information, but as evidence of how institutions classify conduct, assign significance, and communicate expectations.

Through Convera Strategies, Ryan brings this research orientation into public scholarship, speaking, facilitated conversation, organizational inquiry, and project-based professional work. Convera is organized around three intersecting areas: Social Perspectives, Workplace Culture, and Organizational Strategy.
