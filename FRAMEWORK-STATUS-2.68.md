# Framework Status — 2.68.0

## Unified Light/Dark Theme and Contrast Correction

- Light and dark modes now share a semantic color system for page backgrounds, surfaces, text, muted copy, links, accents, borders, cards, and primary actions.
- The homepage now resolves its major text, surface, card, link, and border colors through the selected theme rather than relying on fixed light-mode values followed by scattered dark overrides.
- First-time visitors follow their operating-system color preference; a visitor's explicit Convera theme selection remains persistent and takes priority on later visits.
- Intentionally dark founder-image overlays and the footer remain visually stable in both themes.
- The validated 2.67 responsive hero correction and existing operational controls remain unchanged.
