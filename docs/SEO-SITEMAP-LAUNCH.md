# SEO & Sitemap Launch Review — 2.30

## Public indexable routes

Confirm these resolve successfully and are intended for indexing:

- `/`
- `/services/`
- `/publications/`
- `/community/`
- `/about/`
- `/contact/`
- `/speaking/`
- `/press-kit/`
- `/follow/`

## Utility/private routes

Thank-you, Intake, operator, project portal, proposal, invoice, agreement, and other private/transactional routes should not be promoted in the public sitemap merely because they exist.

## Per-page review

For each public page confirm:

- one descriptive title;
- one useful meta description;
- canonical URL;
- one visible H1;
- sensible heading hierarchy;
- social title/description where applicable;
- keyboard-accessible links and controls;
- image alt text when informative imagery is present;
- no accidental `noindex` on public pages;
- no private identifiers or tokens in canonical/social URLs.

## Structured data

Keep Organization/Person/Article/Service structured data tied to claims actually visible and supportable on the site. Do not imply endorsements, credentials, awards, clients, or affiliations beyond what can be publicly substantiated.
