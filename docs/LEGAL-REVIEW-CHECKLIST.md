# Agreement Template Review Checklist

Before production use, have the agreement terms reviewed for Convera's actual business structure and engagement types. The software exposes fields for these topics but does not decide the legal language for you.

Review at minimum:

- Correct legal names of Convera and client.
- Authority of the signer.
- Scope, deliverables, exclusions, dependencies, and acceptance criteria.
- Fee model, deposit, invoicing/payment timing, expenses, late-payment handling if used, and taxes if relevant.
- Client responsibilities and access to information/personnel.
- Confidentiality and treatment of client information.
- Data handling, security expectations, retention, and any sensitive-data restrictions.
- Intellectual-property ownership, licenses, pre-existing materials, and reuse rights.
- Publicity/portfolio/reference permissions if desired.
- Cancellation, termination, suspension, and effects of termination.
- Change-control process and effect of signed Change Orders.
- Warranties/disclaimers, indemnification, limitation of liability, and insurance requirements where appropriate.
- Independent-contractor relationship.
- Governing law, venue/dispute process, and notice provisions.
- Electronic signature/counterpart language appropriate to the chosen signature service.
- Special procurement requirements for public agencies, universities, nonprofits, or enterprise clients.

The operator should mark a draft “Approved for Use” only after the terms are ready for the intended engagement.
