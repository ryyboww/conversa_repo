# Public Visibility Strategy — 2.29

## Objective

Make it easier for the right audiences to understand Ryan's work, retain a connection to it, invite him into public conversation, and begin professional correspondence without turning Convera into a generic sales funnel.

## Public paths

### Speaking & Media
A single page for organizers, journalists, producers, universities, professional groups, and podcast hosts. It directs all invitations to Contact and preserves manual review.

### Press Kit
Approved biography, fact sheet, editorial guidance, and printable/downloadable text. It reduces repeated requests for basic descriptive material and limits accidental overstatement of credentials.

### Follow the Work
A low-frequency audience-retention pathway for readers who want future essays, research, speaking updates, or substantive Convera developments.

### Publication CTA
A reusable, restrained block for Convera-hosted publications. It connects intellectual attention to three next actions: follow, speaking/media, or Contact.

## Boundaries

- No public Intake link.
- No automatic project creation from public visibility forms.
- No claims of institutional endorsement.
- No silent marketing enrollment.
- No visitor fingerprinting.
- No mandatory newsletter modal or interruption.
- No AI-generated founder portrait.
- No activated Podcast/Institute merely to imply scale.
