# Framework Status 2.65.0

## Status

Release 2.65.0 reorganizes Convera Strategies around a clearer public position, a light-first visual system, and a broader support pathway. It does not change private client workflows, portal security, or production credentials.

## Public experience

- Light mode is the default; visitor-selected dark mode remains available and persistent.
- The homepage explains what Convera does before presenting publications or support.
- Areas of Inquiry appear immediately after the hero and positioning statement.
- Six engagement cards connect institutional strategy, records analysis, organizational culture, communication, speaking, and support.
- The approved Convera Approach text appears unchanged on the homepage and About page.

## Support pathway

- Support the Work remains the site-wide label.
- Reading, sharing, following, referring, and collaborating appear before financial options.
- Financial support is identified as sponsorship and remains separate from professional-service fees.

## Preserved baseline

- Contact → manual review → private Intake invitation remains unchanged.
- Client portal activation guardrails remain unchanged.
- Netlify functions, security controls, email workflows, and operational tooling remain unchanged.
- The Operations Manual remains a separate controlled workstream.
