# Convera Strategies — Content Architecture

## Locked brand identity

**Convera Strategies**  
**Social Perspectives · Workplace Culture · Organizational Strategy**

Convera is presented as a founder-led business and public-facing platform. Research is not used as the default description of Convera's work. Publications may draw on scholarship, records, data, observation, professional experience, and lived experience, but the site does not imply every project follows academic research conventions.

## Homepage

Purpose: establish Convera as a credible business with a personal founder presence, visible mission, services, featured publication, community participation, and clear contribution pathway.

Core sequence:
1. Hero — people, perspective, resilient future
2. Mission + Support actions
3. Featured Publication + contribution panel
4. Social Perspectives / Workplace Culture / Organizational Strategy
5. Work With Convera commercial call-to-action
6. Long-form Ryan Brown quote
7. Community invitation
8. Final support band

The larger “Moment of Transition” / axial tone remains strongest on Mission so the homepage stays clear and uncluttered.

## Mission

Purpose: explain why Convera exists.

Core ideas:
- people and institutions are interconnected
- the present is a moment of transition
- protecting institutions does not mean protecting every existing practice
- institutional strength requires adaptation, trust, responsibility, and usefulness
- securing and protecting institutions is investment in the future

## Services

Convera launches with three broad areas:

### Social Perspectives
Broader social, institutional, community, and relational context surrounding complex challenges.

### Workplace Culture
Communication, expectations, relationships, trust, recurring friction, and organizational language.

### Organizational Strategy
Problem clarification, priorities, options, decisions, and practical next steps.

Initial manageable engagements:
- Focused Strategy Session
- Workplace Culture Review
- Organizational Analysis & Problem Framing
- Communication & Document Review

Convera does not present itself as a law firm, licensed HR practice, mental-health practice, or formal employee-investigation service.

## Publications

Purpose: demonstrate ideas, analysis, public writing, and intellectual development without turning Convera into an academic research site.

Launch publications are limited to completed publicly supportable work. Drafts remain off the public page until ready.

## Community

Community is a core platform feature, not a decorative add-on.

Pathways:
- Share an Observation
- Recommend a Source
- Suggest a Question
- Collaborate

Community participation is intentionally separate from paid client work.

## Support

Support remains highly visible because contributions are part of the founder-led model.

The site distinguishes:
- **Contributions:** voluntary support for publications, public-facing work, community participation, and Convera's development.
- **Services:** paid professional engagements with defined scope and expectations.

Contributions are not represented as tax-deductible charitable donations.

## About

Purpose: make Convera personal and transparent rather than presenting a fictional large firm.

Ryan Brown is identified openly as Founder. The approved real portrait should remain prominent.

## 1.8.0 launch-activation additions

- The approved homepage sequence remains locked; no additional Axial Age block is added to the homepage.
- Mission retains the strongest explicit “A Moment of Transition” language.
- Paid work, general/community correspondence, and voluntary support remain three distinct visitor pathways.

- Newsletter infrastructure remains preserved behind the centralized `newsletter` feature flag and remains off for launch until an email workflow is intentionally activated.
